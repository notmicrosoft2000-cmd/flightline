Two separate voice sets

Kasa uses the standard GPWS and ATC-style voice pack. Gina has its own callouts, alarms and radio phrases, including decision-height callouts and its own windshear and TCAS wording.

While you fly Gina, the mod does not play Kasa voices. The cockpit alarms use separate playback timers so multiple warnings stay audible instead of blocking each other.

Where these sounds appear

  • Radio altimeter callouts at 2500, 1000, 500 and lowering minima.
  • TCAS traffic, climb, descend and clear-of-conflict advisories.
  • GPWS pull-up, sink rate, terrain, gear, flaps, glideslope and windshear.
  • Caution and urgent alarms for engine fire, failure, hydraulic and pressurisation events.
  • Engine loops while aircraft power is available.
