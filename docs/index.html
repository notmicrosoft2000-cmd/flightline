What's in the mod

Everything runs through server-authoritative flight state.

P

Real flight feel

Look up or down to pitch, bank with A/D, manage throttle, flaps, gear, spoilers and airbrakes. Airspeed, stall, overspeed, weather and glide performance all matter.

E

Emergencies with procedures

Engine fires, failures, hydraulic and gear faults, cabin altitude, ice and fuel emergencies come with readable checklists and a recommended next fix.

A

ATC and multiplayer

Operators can track aircraft, issue clearances, and even hand player aircraft to ATC autopilot. Radar traffic and MAYDAY calls keep the sky coordinated.

M

Multiple airframes

Fly the Kasa A380 or the Gina 787. Each has its own speed envelope, engine groups, fuel capacity and cockpit voice alerts.

Quick install

  1. Install Fabric Loader for Minecraft 26.3.
  2. Add Fabric API and Java 25.
  3. Drop the Flight Line jar into your mods folder.
