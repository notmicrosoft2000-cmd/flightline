# Flight Line Code Audit

Follow-up to the static review. `./gradlew build` passes; this includes the automated tests. In-game/client-server checks below remain necessary.

## Addressed

- Ground traffic no longer qualifies for airborne TCAS resolution advisories. Mob avoidance also considers only airborne player aircraft.
- ND AIR/GND counts now come from the server's 128-block traffic scope rather than only client-tracked entities. Radar blips are still drawn from client-tracked entities; the nearest threat/contact remains server-reported.
- The HUD now fits long labels/values and uses a narrow-screen layout; the lower instrument panel and remappable bottom-right key sheet are omitted when there is insufficient room. The PFD adds ILS deviation needles.
- Crash effects use particles and radial knockback instead of an explosion, avoiding terrain and nearby-block damage.
- Flight input is suspended and queued key presses are discarded while a GUI is open, including the inventory and autopilot screens.
- H opens the flight deck before the flight-enabled input gate. Its MCP, NAV/ILS, and key-reference pages allow GUI-based autopilot and ILS configuration; the server validates glide-slope values.
- Recorded cockpit voices replace the placeholder alerts, and alert/callout sounds are queued so clips do not overlap. Continuous TCAS traffic audio plays once until it clears; other warnings retain cooldown repeats. Descent callouts include 2,500 and 1,000 feet.
- Pull-up warnings are restricted to airborne low-altitude descents or projected terrain conflicts; TCAS advisories require both aircraft to be airborne. Ground gear selection is rejected with a one-shot cockpit warning. Steep bank commands are available and sustained bank beyond 65 degrees causes a crash.
- Gear-up and low-flap warnings now require a low-altitude descent, avoiding the persistent “TOO LOW GEAR” alert during level flight.
- Touchdown logic now allows a safe landing, a survivable hard landing with gear damage, and a crash for gear-up or excessive-speed/sink-rate impacts. Aircraft crashes use a custom localized death message.
- Throttle lever and engine spool response are faster. Pushback now explicitly completes forward deceleration before reversing, avoiding a direction-change threshold oscillation, and accelerates to a higher but capped tug speed.
- The radio altimeter has a larger digital readout, color-coded low-altitude tape, altitude ticks, and vertical-speed trend.
- The HUD key reference is now a smaller, borderless vertical list at the bottom right; mappings are gray normally and white while pressed. PFD pitch/roll and ILS readouts have separated, width-limited rows. The MCP shows active heading, altitude, vertical-speed, airspeed, and autothrottle deadband targets.
- `/enableflight` reports failure for dead, spectator, or riding players instead of returning a false success.
- Mob enrollment excludes NoAI mobs, passengers, and vehicles; collision checks query nearby players instead of scanning the full player list for each mob.
- The NAV/ILS panel supports ILS-based autoland. The server requires airborne active ILS coverage, serviceable gear, a running engine, and at least stall speed; it tracks localizer/glideslope targets, manages approach configuration, and enables rollout braking after touchdown. It cancels if guidance is lost and remains manually cancelable after approach conditions deteriorate. Runway setup accepts X/Z plus heading and infers threshold height from terrain; no autoland keybind is used.
- Camera shake is rotational and smoothed, with intensity from gusts/windshear, takeoff, bank angle, low descent, and landing rollout.
- Weather sampling varies wind, turbulence, and estimated visibility across clear, rainy, and thunderstorm conditions. Turbulence affects aircraft velocity/bank and camera shake; thunderstorms produce periodic windshear events and warnings. EICAS and a new H > WX page show weather conditions, while the ND contact range scales to estimated visibility.
- Cockpit alert playback is serialized and supports ordered clip sequences. Terrain/terrain/pull-up and terrain/terrain are composed from the updated clips; radio-altimeter numbers and RETARD use the ding, and caution-class alerts use the caution tone. The gear word recording currently supplied is only 2.4 ms, and no separate “too low” recording exists, so the existing complete TOO LOW GEAR asset remains in use.
- Flight Line keybinds now use Minecraft 26.3 SDL scancodes (`InputConstants.KEY_*`) rather than ASCII values, so defaults map to the intended keys. The requested scheme is implemented: G gear, W/S throttle, E start engines then inventory, H autopilot, K pushback, M brakes, A/D rudder, C/V flaps, X spoilers. Bank and spoiler-extend/retract duplicates were removed; spoilers are a single toggle.
- E and G are consumed in `START_CLIENT_TICK`, before vanilla `Minecraft.handleKeybinds`. E starts the engines while they are off/starting and lets the vanilla inventory key work once an engine is running; G is reserved for gear so the vanilla quick-actions dialog does not open while flight mode is active.
- Old numeric Flight Line keybind values are abandoned by renaming their translation keys, and the legacy migration/auto-reassignment code was removed instead of preserving broken saved bindings.
- The controls screen now detects Escape and Backspace with scancode constants; the previous GLFW keycode checks could not cancel or unbind. It also lists the vanilla throttle/rudder/inventory mappings with flight-specific labels.
- Aircraft impacts no longer carve terrain blocks, matching the particle-and-knockback crash behavior. The flight-radar init dead branch, duplicate warning-label computation, unused session wind counter, and ambiguous spoiler toggle server path were cleaned up.
- `VanillaKeybindMixin` intentionally suppresses vanilla quick actions (G), advancements (L), social interactions (P), friends (O), save/load hotbar (C/X), drop (Q), and offhand swap (F) while flight mode is active to prevent conflicting vanilla inputs.
- `E` starts engines on the ground until running, then opens the inventory normally. `J` toggles engines.
- In-flight HUD clutter was addressed: PFD base width set to 168 with max 0.70x scale, EICAS scaled to 0.75x, and ND radar hidden by default (`showRadar = false`) so player forward visibility is clear. The 12-row vertical keybinds reference list on the bottom-right HUD was restored and made legible, while the temporary horizontal hotbar chip was removed.
- `AutopilotScreen` (`H`) MCP was remade into an authentic airliner Mode Control Panel with 5 horizontal bays (`A/T · SPEED`, `HDG SEL`, `ALT HOLD`, `VERT SPEED`, `A/P CMD`), green LED annunciator light bars, recessed amber seven-segment display windows, step adjusters, and lower live guidance deviation tapes. Dead pages (WX/SYS with 0 controls, airliner FPLN filing) were removed.
- `EmergencyScreen` (`O`) was completely remade into a clean, compact 580x370 layout with prominent `[T]` recommended action execution, active condition cards with countdown bars, and categorized procedure groups (Engines, Airframe & Hydraulics, Emergency Operations).
- Dynamic camera pitch follow and aircraft attitude rendering: `FlightCameraMixin` dynamically tracks the aircraft's aerodynamic pitch attitude with smooth inertia without fighting mouse input, and `LivingEntityRendererMixin` tilts player and mob 3D models with pitch (`Axis.XP, -pitch`) to match bank roll.
- `ScreenScaler` enforces a readable scale floor (0.68f) and prefers 1.0f rendering when fit allows, preventing microscopic sub-pixel fonts.
- The full-screen warning alarm flash was softened into a sleek 2px edge vignette.
- The interactive tutorial's `GO TO THIS STEP` action deep-links the first unfinished objective to its MCP, NAV/ILS, radar, gear, flaps, or engines panel.
- Operators can force a dimension-scoped windshear with `/flightline weather windshear [durationTicks]` (default `140`) during active thunderstorms and clear it with `/flightline weather clear`.
- Flight attitude, turn-rate, ground-effect, angle-of-attack, and TCAS closest-approach/resolution math now lives in shared production helpers; the safety and physics tests exercise those helpers instead of recomputing the formulas locally.

## Remaining Risks / Verification

1. **Client radar blips remain limited by vanilla entity tracking.** The server scope counts and nearest contact are authoritative, but the client only draws individual blips for entities it knows about. A future contact-list payload would be needed for all server-visible contacts to appear as blips.
2. **Mob physics intentionally overrides eligible mobs' movement while native AI stays enabled.** NoAI mobs, riders, and vehicles are excluded and gravity is restored, but unusual modded mobs may still have incompatible movement assumptions.
3. **Mob processing still scans loaded entities every server tick.** Nearby player collision queries were narrowed, but large loaded-mob populations should be profiled in a stress world.
4. **ILS references are session-only.** Set one in the H > NAV/ILS page at the runway threshold while facing down the runway, or use `/flightline ils set [glideSlope]`; clear it with the GUI or `/flightline ils clear`. Verify the sign and capture behavior in-game.
5. **Context-sensitive E/G handling and key suppression need a live-client check.** Verify E starts engines when they are off, stays suppressed during the start sequence, opens inventory once running, and starts them again after shutdown. Verify G toggles gear without opening vanilla quick actions. With flight mode on, confirm G/L/O/P/C/X/Q/F no longer trigger vanilla actions; with flight mode off, confirm all still behave normally. Mixin injection is only proven by launching the client.
6. **Collision behavior needs a live server check.** Verify knockback, particles, mob/player collision behavior, and that blocks are not damaged.
7. **Landing and crash behavior need a live check.** Verify safe and hard landings, gear-up/excessive-sink crashes, nonlethal hard-landing damage, and the localized death message.
8. **Throttle and pushback response need live checks.** Verify throttle/spool acceleration and deceleration remain controllable, pushback from a stop and while taxiing does not reverse abruptly, and brakes still take precedence.
9. **Voice timing and loudness need a live-client check.** Verify clips play at natural speed and the intended louder level, terrain/pull-up speech starts immediately without a caution-tone delay, the too-low-terrain callout wins over generic terrain, the composed phrase plays in sequence, and clips do not overlap or clip.
10. **HUD and flight-deck placement need live-client checks** at standard and small GUI resolutions, including ILS needles, pitch/roll readouts, radio-altimeter ticks/trend, ND counts, the 13-row key hint panel, the six-tab flight deck, 2×2 MCP hitboxes and drafts, ALERT wording, the wordless edge-flash onset, TCAS advisory isolation, and tutorial deep links.
11. **Autoland needs live-client/server verification.** Confirm the arming gates and ILS capture, localizer/glideslope tracking, automatic gear/flap/speed configuration, cancel and ILS-loss behavior, landing survivability, and rollout braking. Geometry tests do not model a complete world landing.
12. **Camera shake needs visual comfort checks** during gusts, windshear, takeoff, steep banks, low descent, and rollout; verify reset on disconnect and that the smoothed effect feels consistent at different frame rates.
13. **Weather needs live checks** in clear, rainy, and thunderstorm worlds: compare wind/turbulence/visibility readouts, confirm the ND scope changes, and verify a thunderstorm windshear warning/audio plus flight response.
14. **Remaining audio gaps.** Stall, engine, fuel, gear-system, takeoff-configuration, and overspeed warnings currently have a caution tone but no spoken phrase; gear/too-low word clips should be added when valid recordings are available. Confirm the caution cue still reads clearly under the louder voice level.

Use `./gradlew runClient` when assets/display support are available; a successful compile alone does not validate mixin injection, key event order, HUD placement, or movement synchronization.
